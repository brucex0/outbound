Title: Plainstride
Short description: Adaptive running plans, live coaching, GPS activity tracking and progress.
Full description: Find your stride with a running companion that helps you decide what to do today, guides you through your activity, and makes your progress easier to understand.

TRAIN WITH A PLAN THAT FITS YOUR LIFE
Build a practical training plan around your goals and experience. See your next workout, understand its purpose, and adjust as your training and feedback evolve.

RECORD YOUR ACTIVITIES
Track runs, walks, hikes, and bike rides with GPS. Follow your time, distance, and pace, save your activity history, and add photos to remember the moments along the way.

GET GUIDANCE AS YOU MOVE
Choose timely spoken coaching and activity guidance. Keep coaching controls in your hands and focus on your next step.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
Explore training trends, personal bests, and recent activity highlights. Turn your history into a clearer picture of how your training is developing.

STAY CONNECTED
Connect with other runners, share the activities you choose, and organize activities together. Optional live sharing lets invited people follow your activity and send encouragement.

YOUR CHOICES COME FIRST
Location, health connections, camera, microphone, notifications, social sharing, and live sharing are optional and requested in context. You choose what to connect and share.

Plainstride supports fitness and general wellness. It does not provide medical advice or replace professional care.
