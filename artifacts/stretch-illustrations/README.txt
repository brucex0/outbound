Dynamic entries alternate for a total interval unless explicitly marked each side.

English, Chinese, Spanish app instructions are retained in mapping.json for imported movements. Original source references are retained for the initial 50. New images were generated with built-in ImageGen and converted with VTracer. Prompts/pose briefs and master paths are in mapping.json.
